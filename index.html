About

From Junagadh to Ahmedabad, with a notebook of songs

I grew up in Junagadh and now live in Ahmedabad. I started writing songs at a very young age, and I earned my engineering degree in Information Technology in my home town.

For more than eight years I have sold custom software, cloud and SaaS services to companies around the world. My engineering background lets me stay with a deal from the first call through solution design, negotiation and long-term account growth.

In 2020 I released Samaj, my first original song, written and performed by me. Music, especially hip-hop, is still what I care about most outside work, along with other art forms.
