About

From Junagadh to Ahmedabad, with a notebook of songs

I grew up in Junagadh and now live in Ahmedabad. I started writing songs at a very young age, then studied Information Technology at Gujarat Technological University.

Since 2018 I have worked in business development for software companies. My work sits where app and website development, design, marketing and sales meet.

In 2020 I released Samaj, my first original song, written and performed by me. Music, especially hip-hop, is still what I care about most outside work, along with other art forms.
